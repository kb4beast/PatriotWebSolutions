The learning path

One live hour. Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Trained to standard, not to time.

We teach military members, veterans, and their families in Killeen, Texas. Progress means demonstrating a skill — a GO — not surviving a deadline. A NO-GO means we retrain it. “Train to standard, not to time” is borrowed from military training on purpose: the calendar bends, the standard does not, and no one gets left behind.

Inside one hour

Our default session plan. We publish a real session log once cohorts run.

  1. 10 minBrief & questions — last session’s skill, retried cold
  2. 35 minGuided build — everyone works the same real task while the instructor circulates and the AI tutor adapts the exercise to each learner
  3. 15 minDemonstrate & AAR — one learner shows the work; everyone logs what held up and what needs a retrain (an after-action review, in plain terms)

Human instruction, AI-tailored practice

A person teaches every class and sets the standard. Alongside the instructor, an AI tutor adapts each cohort’s lesson plan to each participant — the pace, the examples, and the difficulty of the practice change with the learner, which is one-to-one attention no single teacher can give a whole room at once.

Everything you produce is checked and returned with specific feedback: homework, tests, task briefs, documented workflows, the skills and agents you build. Feedback names what worked, what did not, and the fix to try next. The instructor reviews the record and makes the GO / NO-GO call on every skill.

Learner work stays private to the learner and the instructor. What goes into any AI tool follows the red lines you write in week two.

The readiness standard

Train to standard, not to time: the calendar bends, the standard does not. Demonstrations are private and untimed; a NO-GO schedules a retrain, and nothing about a retrain is recorded against you. Crawl, walk, run.

After foundations: the core path

  • Core · 4–6 weeks12–18 live hours

    AI foundations and safe use

    The six-week syllabus above, paced to the cohort. Use an AI tool for a real task, learn privacy basics, source checking, and when a person should make the decision.

  • Core · 6–8 weeks18–24 live hours

    Prompting, research, and verification

    Turn a vague request into a clear brief, compare outputs, trace sources, and revise based on evidence.

  • Core · 6–8 weeks18–24 live hours

    Reusable assistants, skills, and current plugins

    Organize instructions, examples, tests, knowledge, and tool access. Learn how today’s publishing routes differ from older GPT and plugin formats.

  • Core · 4–6 weeks12–18 live hours

    Career project and portfolio clinic

    Map job descriptions to evidence, write a truthful case study, explain tradeoffs, and practice an interview based on work you actually completed.

Core planning range: 20–28 instructional weeks

That equals 60–84 live hours before repeats, breaks, absences, or prerequisite support. It is a planning range, not a promised graduation date.

Optional advanced paths

  • Optional · 10–14 weeks30–42 live hours

    APIs and connected workflows

    Structured data, test credentials, error handling, rate limits, cost controls, and safe connections between tools.

  • Optional · 12–16 weeks36–48 live hours

    Agents, tools, and retrieval

    Bounded permissions, trusted knowledge, checkpoints, recovery, and human review for consequential actions.

  • Optional · 12–16 weeks36–48 live hours

    Evaluation, replay, and reliable improvement

    Baselines, held-out cases, point-in-time replay, leakage, contamination, reward hacking, overfitting, and honest comparison.

We teach replay, leakage, contamination, reward hacking, and backtesting so learners can detect and prevent them. Every learner signs the same commitment in the community agreement: evaluation methods find weaknesses — they never manipulate scores or deceive people.

A learner pursuing every optional path would plan for roughly 54–74 instructional weeks and 162–222 live hours, often longer. You do not need every track to benefit.
