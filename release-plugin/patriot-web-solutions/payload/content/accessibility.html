Accessibility

Access is part of the work.

Patriot Web Solutions aims for content and interactions that work with keyboards, screen readers, zoom, reduced motion, captions, and common mobile devices. We use clear structure, visible focus, readable contrast, text alternatives, and plain instructions as baseline practices.

Ask for help or report a barrier

Email support@patriotwebsolutions.org or call (254) 761-5991. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it. Do not include medical details in a public form. We will provide a private channel when more information is needed.

Ongoing review

Automated checks help find some barriers; they do not replace testing with people. The release process includes keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, error-message, and mobile checks. We will date material improvements and known limitations as testing continues.
