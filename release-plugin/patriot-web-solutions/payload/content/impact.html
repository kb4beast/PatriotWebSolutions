What we count when we report

Access: distinct learners, live hours delivered, attendance, and repeat support. Skills: private readiness demonstrations and completed artifacts. Outcomes: projects shipped and voluntary career follow-up, reported with denominators and limits — teaching activity is never presented as an employment result.

[pws_fact key="first_report_target" prefix="First report target: " fallback="The first reporting period is set when the first cohort has a start date; the target date is published here, not before."]

Our reporting rules

  • [pws_icon name="check"]Every number has a definition, owner, reporting period, source, and verification date.
  • [pws_icon name="check"]Participant stories require informed permission and can be withdrawn from future publication.
  • [pws_icon name="check"]Small groups are protected when details could identify someone.
  • [pws_icon name="check"]Unknown history stays unknown; a missing baseline is never displayed as zero.
  • [pws_icon name="check"]We distinguish what we delivered from what changed afterward.

What we will not claim yet

Each line names its unlock evidence. These entries move into the ledger above only after that evidence exists.

  • [pws_icon name="x"]Employment outcomes. Unlock: voluntary, dated learner follow-up with denominators.
  • [pws_icon name="x"]Testimonials and stories. Unlock: informed written permission, revocable, in the speaker’s own words.
  • [pws_icon name="x"]Historical totals from earlier program years. Unlock: source records with definitions and date ranges a reviewer could re-check.
  • [pws_icon name="x"]Learners served, hours delivered. Unlock: the first cohort actually running, with attendance records.
