Impact

Show the work. Explain the measure.

Patriot Web Solutions is rebuilding its impact record around evidence people can inspect. Historical totals and testimonials will appear only after their source, definition, date range, and publication permission are verified.

Access

Distinct learners served, live hours delivered, attendance, repeat support, and whether people could use the learning environment.

Skills

Private readiness demonstrations, completed artifacts, independent checks, and what learners can explain or do without prompting.

Outcomes

Projects shipped and voluntary career follow-up, reported with denominators and limits. Teaching activity is not presented as an employment result.

Our reporting rules

  • Every number has a definition, owner, reporting period, source, and verification date.
  • Participant stories require informed permission and can be withdrawn from future publication.
  • Small groups are protected when details could identify someone.
  • Unknown history stays unknown; a missing baseline is never displayed as zero.
  • We distinguish what we delivered from what changed afterward.
