Privacy notice

This notice explains the information Patriot Web Solutions expects to handle through this website. It must be checked against the live site’s final analytics, donation, account, email, and hosting configuration before publication.

Donation and account systems may collect additional information under their own displayed notices.

How information is used

We use form information to respond, understand program interest, plan services, prevent abuse, and maintain necessary records. We do not use an inquiry as permission to publish a story or testimonial. A separate permission process is required.

Service providers and recipients

Website hosting, email delivery, security, analytics if enabled, and donation/payment services may process information needed to provide their functions. Payment-card details should be handled by the configured payment processor and should not be sent through contact or interest forms. The final provider list, roles, international transfers, and retention settings must match the live configuration.

Cookies and technical information

WordPress, security, donation, account, and measurement tools may use cookies or similar storage for sessions, preferences, fraud prevention, and site operation. Optional analytics or advertising measurement must not be enabled until consent requirements and actual vendors are documented.

Retention and choices

Records should be kept only as long as needed for the request, legal obligations, security, accounting, or documented program operations. You may ask about access, correction, or deletion by emailing support@patriotwebsolutions.org. Some records may need to be retained for legal, transaction, fraud-prevention, or backup purposes. Applicable rights depend on your location and the organization’s obligations.

Children and sensitive information

Age eligibility and guardian participation rules must be confirmed before youth enrollment opens. This website is not designed to collect sensitive military, medical, or identity documents through public forms.

Security and changes

Reasonable safeguards reduce risk but no system is perfectly secure. Material changes will be dated on this page. Questions can be sent to support@patriotwebsolutions.org or mailed to Patriot Web Solutions, Killeen, Texas 76549.
