The interest list is how cohorts form. It is not enrollment and creates no payment obligation — dates, format, seats, and any costs reach this list in writing before anyone commits to anything.

Who this is for

Service members, veterans, and military family members, beginners included. If that is not you but the mission fits, write to us anyway — supporters and community partners have a place here too.

What happens after you submit

  1. We read the subject areas you selected. Nothing is automated; a person reads every entry.
  2. We contact you when a matching orientation or cohort is forming, with dates, format, and expectations in writing.
  3. You decide. Schedule, format, and any costs are confirmed before any commitment.

Need an accessibility accommodation? Ask for a private conversation in the message field, and please keep medical details out of this form.
