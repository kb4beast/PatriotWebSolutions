About

The record behind Patriot Web Solutions.

Patriot Web Solutions began by helping military-connected people learn website skills. The tools changed. The purpose did not: patient instruction, useful work, and a fair chance to build confidence through practice.

The pivot

We reshaped the program around practical AI: how to use it, question it, build with it, and explain the results. Family members belong in this work on their own standing — experience with transitions, logistics, communication, caregiving, and problem-solving is capability, and we treat it that way.

How we work fits in one paragraph, not a values grid: we repeat and reframe until a skill is demonstrated; we show sources, tests, and limits; we protect privacy and never turn service into decoration; and every lesson connects to a real task someone actually has.

President and founder

Brian Espinosa

President & founder

  • Software engineer

Brian Espinosa founded Patriot Web Solutions and serves as its president. He is a software engineer by trade and a family man first. His view of AI shapes the program: it can be a better one-to-one tutor than any single teacher can be to a room of students — so a human instructor leads and sets the standard, while an AI tutor tailors the practice to each participant and every piece of work comes back graded with real feedback.

He is also the owner of record for every fact on this site. Corrections go to support@patriotwebsolutions.org.
